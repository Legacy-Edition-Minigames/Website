  # Welcome back.
  You may have noticed, there's quite a few changes.

  After careful consideration internally, we have decided to change the direction of the project.

  We have decided to move away from using vanilla maps and ONLY use custom maps made by the community.

  # Why was this decision made?
  Making an accurate remake of the minigames takes a *lot* of effort and we will not settle for just making a low quality barely functioning remake.

  <div style="font-size: 0.70em;">It would be a real shame if someone did that...<br></div>

  To maintain our quality standard without having to worry about this whole "accuracy" nonsense (and to save us a *lot* of effort), we have decided to only use custom maps.

  # What does this mean for the project?

  It means improvements.

  - Better map design
  - More balanced chest placement
  - Encouraging creativity
  - 

  # Will you reconsider?
  ### No.
  Infact, we will be applying these changes **immediately**. In the past we have failed to follow through with our previous major changes and we can't risk having that happen again.

  If you would like to make your own custom map, check out our [documentation](https://docs.legacyminigames.net)! We hope to see what creations you will all make.
